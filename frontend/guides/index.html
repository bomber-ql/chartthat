Charts That Sell

Eight field notes on making data close deals — no chart-beauty contests, just what works in front of a client.

I've made slides for a living for years. Everything here comes from the same observation: clients don't buy charts, they buy the conclusion a chart makes impossible to argue with. The notes run in three layers — the story your deck tells, the charts that carry it, and the data that feeds them. Published notes are linked; the rest are being written between pitches.
